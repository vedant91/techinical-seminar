# Acme Q2 2025 Financial Report

Quarterly financial report for Acme, covering the second quarter of fiscal
year 2025. Figures are in US dollars unless otherwise noted.

## 1. Executive Summary

Revenue grew twenty two percent quarter over quarter, driven by enterprise
adoption of the AI Platform. Operating margin improved as infrastructure costs
scaled below revenue growth. The company added forty one new enterprise
customers during the quarter.

## 2. Revenue

Revenue by tier for the quarter:

| Tier | Q2 2025 | Q1 2025 | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $1.2M | $1.1M | 9% |
| Growth | $3.4M | $3.0M | 13% |
| Enterprise | $8.9M | $6.6M | 35% |
| Total | $13.5M | $10.7M | 26% |

Enterprise revenue now represents sixty six percent of total revenue. Annual
recurring revenue reached $51 million at quarter end.

## 3. Operating Costs

Infrastructure spending rose eighteen percent quarter over quarter. The
largest cost drivers were GPU capacity for the retrieval engine and expanded
storage for document libraries. Headcount grew by twelve engineers, focused on
the validation pipeline and the hybrid retrieval engine.

## 4. Guidance

For the next quarter, Acme expects revenue between $14.5 million and $15.5
million. Guidance assumes continued enterprise growth and stable churn in the
Starter tier. Management will re-evaluate guidance after the upcoming pricing
review.
